# Safety rules (read before every build)

1. **Never touch mains voltage (220V).** Any mains switching uses a certified, enclosed relay module or smart plug, checked by a qualified electrician or lab instructor. Prototype at 5V-12V first.
2. **LiPo / Li-ion:** charge only on a non-flammable surface, never unattended. Use protected cells and a BMS.
3. **Soldering:** ventilate, tie back hair, never leave the iron on.
4. **Read the datasheet** of every part: voltage, current limits, pinout - before wiring.
5. **Fuse your battery packs** and double-check polarity before connecting power.
