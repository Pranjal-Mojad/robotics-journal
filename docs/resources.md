# Resources

Names come from the two roadmap PDFs. Check availability and prices before relying on them.

## Core library
- Make: Electronics (Charles Platt)
- Practical Electronics for Inventors (Scherz and Monk)
- The Art of Electronics (Horowitz and Hill) - later
- Modern Robotics (Lynch and Park) - free book + lectures
- Feedback Systems (Astrom and Murray) - free
- CS50 / CS50P (Harvard)
- ROS 2 official tutorials + Articulated Robotics (YouTube)
- YouTube: Paul McWhorter, GreatScott!, Ben Eater, Brian Douglas

## Free courses (from PDF 2)
- NPTEL Mechatronics (IIT Roorkee), Design of Mechatronic Systems (IIT Bombay), Engineering Mechanics (IIT Madras)
- MIT OCW 18.06, 18.03, 2.12 Introduction to Robotics
- MITx Circuits and Electronics
- Underactuated Robotics (MIT, Tedrake)
- The Construct Academy (ROS 2)

## Tools
Arduino IDE / PlatformIO, Tinkercad, Git/GitHub, KiCad, Fusion 360 or FreeCAD, Python (NumPy, Matplotlib), Ubuntu/WSL2 (from month 6), ROS 2 + Gazebo (from month 8), OpenCV + MediaPipe (from month 9).

## Starter budget (approximate, verify prices)
Rs 6,000-10,000 to start; Month 1: Arduino, breadboard, component kit, multimeter. Do not buy a 3D printer or Raspberry Pi in month 1.
